During the low-fidelity wireframing process, I identified a few changes that could improve the usability of StudyTime. 

The duration options were changed from 15, 25, and 45 minutes to 15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes, with 30 minutes selected by default. I felt that these intervals made more sense for students because they provide consistent 15-minute increments and allow for both shorter and longer study sessions. 

I also added a Stop Timer button to allow users to intentionally end an unfinished study session. This is separate from resetting the timer, which allows users to restart their session while keeping the same subject. 

Checkboxes were added to the study history so users can select individual sessions and calculate their combined study time. This gives users more flexibility when reviewing their study progress without changing or removing their saved history. 

The narrow-screen layout uses vertically stacked sections, while the wide-screen layout places the timer and study history side by side to make better use of the available screen space.
